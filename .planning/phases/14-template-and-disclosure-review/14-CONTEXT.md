## Inherited confirmed decisions

- **D-01:** Preserve original-template source/content identity separately from the working copy. Classify required structure, normal completion, necessary additions and optional formatting changes with source evidence; differences alone are not defects.
- **D-02:** Produce a restoration checklist with exact source/current locations and supported original text. Missing originals stay unknown; factual declarations and signatures are not automatically affirmed, reconstructed or applied.
- **D-03:** Review TODOs, comments, chat phrases and tool markers in context. Retain required disclosures, original instructions, legitimate incomplete prototypes and experimental AI material; do not perform blanket deletion or infer authorship from style.
- **D-04:** Separate private work records, assignment content and the required disclosure location. Centralize truthful disclosure where the actual requirements permit; do not demand repeated AI labels without source support or erase a specifically required inline attribution.
- **D-05:** Compare known AI uses to the actual disclosure, identify omissions/contradictions/incomplete records, and never invent percentages, manual-only provenance or compliance. A user statement is attributed as such; no record is not proof of no AI use.
- **D-06:** Continue authorized assistance while assessing policy independently. Keep original policy text and stable findings; a working copy omission does not change policy or make a contradictory declaration true.
- **D-07:** Reuse the existing pre-read source gate, current selection and provenance. Explicit exclusions apply to originals and work records as well as drafts; unsupported partial exclusion skips the document group. No automatic history or private-chat collection.
- **D-08:** Integrate into the single Skill's preparation/in_progress/final and generate/review routes. Generated prompts carry relevant evidence and boundaries; requested review yields actual matrices and minimal handoff without additional permission inferred from a prompt.
- **D-09:** Keep delivery local and small: Markdown references and synthetic cases, no editor/regex scrubber, new dependency, provider call, global installation or MCP contract change. Full finding retirement/recheck remains Phase 15.
